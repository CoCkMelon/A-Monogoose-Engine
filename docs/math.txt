ame-next ENGINE SPEC
File: math.txt - pure header-only 2D/3D math (include/ame/math.h)

WHAT THIS IS
Single-header pure value math shared by AME_2D and AME_3D (principles
rule 6): vec2, vec3, vec4, mat3, column-major mat4, unit quaternions,
frustum planes/culling, and Bezier/Catmull-Rom curves. No state, no
allocation, -ffp-contract=off for per-binary determinism, with SSE2
twins for ame_m4_mul and ame_quat_mul bit-identical to their scalar
bodies (proven in tests/test_math_cglm.c against cglm 0.9).

S1 DEAD-MATH RESOLUTION (2026-11-21, issue #6)
The API census found five public math functions with no external
caller and no unit test. Decisions per function:
  ame_m3_identity    KEEP + TESTED (tests/test_math.c): needed by S15
                     skeleton/normal-matrix math.
  ame_quat_identity  KEEP + TESTED (tests/test_math.c): needed by S15
                     rest poses and clip sampling.
  ame_quat_norm      KEEP + TESTED (tests/test_math.c): needed by S15
                     quaternion normalization and slerp fallback.
  ame_v2_scale       KEEP + TESTED (tests/test_math.c): used internally
                     by ame_v2_norm; planned consumer S17 kinematic
                     character controller (2D velocity/displacement).
  ame_v4_add         KEEP + TESTED (tests/test_math.c): used internally
                     by ame_v4_lerp; planned consumer S15/S15B 4-weight
                     skinning/RGBA accumulation.

CENSUS (S1, tests-only surface)
  Intended consumers for the math.h functions currently called only in
  tests/: S15 skeleton/animation (ame_quat_*, ame_m3_*, ame_m4_*),
  S15B GPU skinning (ame_v4_*), S17 character controller (ame_v2_*,
  ame_v3_*), S18 camera & culling (ame_frustum_*, ame_m4_*).

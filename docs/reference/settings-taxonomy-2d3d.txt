COMPREHENSIVE GRAPHICS AND PRESENTATION SETTINGS STANDARD - 2D AND 3D

GOAL: Support the widest possible range of hardware, player preference, and accessibility needs. No setting should be preset-only.

DISPLAY AND RESOLUTION
- Monitor Selection: Choose target display in multi-monitor setups.
- Display Mode: Exclusive Fullscreen, Borderless Fullscreen, Windowed, Borderless Windowed. Remember mode per monitor.
- Resolution: Manual numeric input for Width x Height plus curated presets. Range from 2 to 16384 with validation and clamping. Show native resolution.
- Aspect Ratio: Auto, 4:3, 16:10, 16:9, 21:9, 32:9, Portrait modes, Custom. Option to lock aspect ratio to window.
- Refresh Rate: Manual selection including 60, 120, 144, 240, etc. plus custom.
- Orientation and Safe Zone: Portrait or Landscape, Safe Area or Overscan adjustment sliders.
- Resolution Scaling and Render Scale: Percentage slider 25 percent to 300 percent plus division modes /2, /4 and multiplication x2, x4. Separate Render Resolution and Display Resolution.
- Dynamic Resolution: Toggle, Target Framerate, Min and Max Scale Limit.
- HDR: SDR or HDR toggle, HDR10, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, Peak Brightness in nits, Paper White, Tone-map calibration, Auto HDR.
- Calibration: Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Black Level, Color Temperature, SDR and HDR brightness calibration images.

FRAMERATE AND SYNCHRONIZATION
- Framerate Limit: Direct numeric input for Foreground, Background, Menu, On Battery, Cutscenes. Examples 30, 60, 120, 144, 240.
- Uncapped Framerate: Explicit toggle.
- V-Sync: Off, On, Adaptive, Fast, 1/2 Rate, 1/3 Rate.
- Adaptive Sync: NVIDIA G-Sync, AMD FreeSync, VESA VRR toggle.
- Low Latency: NVIDIA Reflex plus Boost, AMD Anti-Lag plus, Intel Xe Low Latency, Ultra Low Latency Mode.
- Frame Pacing: Force consistent frame delivery intervals; toggle frame-time smoothing and eliminate micro-stutter independent of V-Sync.

ANTI-ALIASING AND UPSCALING
- Traditional AA: Off, FXAA, SMAA, MSAA 2x/4x/8x, TXAA, TAA, CMAA, RSA.
- Modern Upscaling: DLSS 2/3/4, FSR 1/2/3, XeSS 1/2, PSSR, TSR. Allow multiple versions to coexist.
- Upscaler Settings: Mode - Quality, Balanced, Performance, Ultra Performance, Ultra Quality, Native AA, Custom percent, Sharpness slider 0 to 100, Mipmap Bias.
- Frame Generation: DLSS Frame Gen, FSR Frame Gen, XeSS Frame Gen - Toggle plus Multiplier x2, x3, x4.
- Variable Rate Shading (VRS): Toggle and Quality setting.
- Native AA Mode: Render at native resolution with upscaler applied only as anti-aliasing.
- Mipmap Bias: Numeric slider for texture sharpness versus aliasing at distance.

GEOMETRY, LOD AND WORLD DETAIL
- Overall Preset: Plus individual overrides.
- Mesh or Model Quality: Character, NPC, Environment, Vehicle separate sliders.
- Level of Detail: LOD Quality, LOD Distance or Bias, LOD Transition smoothness.
- Draw Distance: View Distance, Object Distance, Character Distance.
- Density: Vegetation Density, Grass Density, Crowd or NPC Density, Traffic Density.
- Tessellation and Displacement: Quality plus Distance, Parallax Occlusion Mapping.
- LOD Transition Smoothness: Crossfade distance and dither pattern for LOD swaps.
- Crowd and Traffic Density: Separate from general NPC density.
- Displacement and Tessellation Distance: How far tessellation remains active.

TEXTURES, MATERIALS AND SHADERS
- Texture Quality: Pool size - Low, Medium, High, Ultra, Insane, or 512 to 8192 or 8K. Maximum texture resolution limit.
- Filtering: Bilinear, Trilinear, Anisotropic 2x/4x/8x/16x, Mipmap Quality, Mipmap Bias.
- Shader or Material Quality: Low to Ultra, affects complexity of shaders.
- PBR: PBR Quality slider for Lighting, Reflections, Specular. For stylized games: Option to disable PBR. For realistic games: PBR should not be fully disabled, only scaled.
- Texture Streaming: Streaming Quality, Pool Budget, Aggressiveness of streaming and eviction.
- Texture Pool Budget: Explicit MB or GB cap with real-time usage bar.
- Shader Quality or Precision: Low or Full precision; affects lighting computation cost.
- Material Complexity: Number of texture channels used, complexity of BRDF approximation.

LIGHTING, SHADOWS, REFLECTIONS AND RAY TRACING
- Shadow Quality: Resolution 512 to 8192, Distance, Cascade Count 1 to 6, Cascade Resolution.
- Shadow Filtering: Soft Shadows, PCF, PCSS - Quality and Sharpness.
- Contact Shadows: Toggle, Quality, Length.
- Ambient Occlusion: Toggle, Type - SSAO, HBAO+, HDAO, GTAO, CACAO, RTAO. Intensity, Radius, Quality.
- Global Illumination: Quality, Bounces, SSGI, RTGI.
- Reflections: Overall Quality, SSR Quality, Resolution, Distance, Planar Reflections, Cubemap Resolution.
- Ray Tracing Master: Toggle plus Preset Low, Medium, High, Ultra.
- RT Features: RT Shadows, RT Reflections, RT Ambient Occlusion, RT Global Illumination, RT Caustics, RT Translucency, Bounce Count.
- Path Tracing: Toggle, Samples Per Pixel, Bounce Count.
- RT Denoiser: Quality, BVH Quality.
- Shadow Distance Scale: Independent from general draw distance.
- Reflection Cube Resolution: Explicit cubemap resolution control.
- RT BVH Quality: Bounding Volume Hierarchy build quality for ray tracing acceleration.
- RT Denoiser Types: Toggle between spatial and temporal denoisers with quality tiers.

VOLUMETRICS, WATER, VEGETATION AND ENVIRONMENT
- Volumetric Quality: Fog, Lighting or God Rays or Light Shafts, Clouds - Quality, Resolution, Distance.
- Water: Quality, Reflection, Refraction, Caustics, Foam, Tessellation, Simulation, Physics simulation tick rate.
- Terrain: Quality, Tessellation, Detail Distance, Heightmap Quality.
- Foliage or Grass: Quality, Density, Distance, Wind Simulation.
- Sky or Atmosphere: Quality, Cloud Quality, Atmospheric Scattering, 2D versus 3D volumetric cloud quality, shadow casting.

PARTICLES, DECALS AND PHYSICS VISUALS
- Particles or VFX: Quality, Count, Lifetime, Lighting.
- Decals: Quality, Count, Lifetime, Blood or Debris persistence, Projection type Orthographic versus projected, clipping.
- Physics Visuals: Cloth Quality, Destruction Quality, Fluid Simulation, Fire or Smoke Quality, Physics Tick Rate for visual updates.
- Weather Particles: Rain, Snow, Dust - Quality and Density.

POST-PROCESSING AND CINEMATIC EFFECTS
- Post-Process Quality: Master quality toggle with per-effect override.
- Bloom: Toggle, Intensity, Threshold, Radius, Quality.
- Depth of Field: Toggle, Quality, Bokeh Shape such as Circular or Hexagonal, Near and Far plane numeric input, Auto-focus toggle, ADS DoF override.
- Motion Blur: Toggle, Camera Motion Blur separate, Per-Object Motion Blur separate, Intensity, Shutter Angle in degrees, Sample Count.
- Lens Effects: Lens Flare, Lens Dirt, Chromatic Aberration Intensity, Vignette Intensity, Film Grain Intensity and Size, Lens Distortion.
- Sharpening: Toggle, Intensity, Type - CAS, LumaSharpen, Unsharp Mask with radius.
- Color Grading: LUT Strength, Tone Mapping - ACES, Neutral, Reinhard, etc.
- Film Grain: Size and Intensity separate controls.

CAMERA SETTINGS
- FOV: Horizontal and Vertical separate sliders for First-Person, Third-Person, Vehicle, ADS or Scoped. Range 60 to 120 plus with numeric input. Hor plus or Vert minus scaling option.
- Clipping: Near Clip and Far Clip numeric input.
- Camera Comfort: Camera Shake Intensity 0 to 100 percent, Head Bob, View Bob, Weapon Bob, Camera Roll, Smoothing.
- Motion Sickness Aids: Center Dot, Peripheral Vignette during movement, FOV vignette disable, Motion Blur disable.
- Camera Smoothing: Interpolation speed for FOV transitions and position smoothing.

2D PIXEL ART AND STYLIZED SPECIFIC
- Sprite Quality: Resolution, Compression.
- Filtering Mode: Point or Nearest Neighbor for Pixel Perfect, Bilinear, Trilinear, xBRZ, HQx, Scale2x, Bicubic.
- Scaling: Integer Scaling Toggle, Pixel Perfect Mode, Aspect Correction for pixel art.
- Retro Filters: CRT Shader - Scanlines, Curvature, Phosphor Bloom, NTSC or Composite artifacting, LCD Grid.
- Animation: Sprite Animation FPS Limiter, Frame Interpolation for 2D, Frame interpolation for low-FPS sprites.
- 2D Effects: Parallax Layer Quality, VFX Pixelation Consistency Toggle, Line Thickness for vector or cartoon games.
- Canvas Resolution: Separate UI or Game render resolution for 2D.
- Sprite Batch Quality: Sorting and batch size limits.

TEXT TYPOGRAPHY SUBTITLES AND LOCALIZATION
- UI Text Scaling: Global slider 50 percent to 200 percent independent of UI Scale. Font Size Override.
- Font Selection: Default, Dyslexia-Friendly - OpenDyslexic, Atkinson Hyperlegible, High-Legibility. Font Weight or Bold Text toggle.
- Text Rendering Quality: Anti-aliasing, SDF or MSDF Sharpness, Hinting, Subpixel rendering.
- Readability: Text Outline Toggle plus Thickness, Drop Shadow Toggle plus Offset, Background Plate or Text Box Opacity, Line Spacing, Letter Spacing.
- Subtitles and Closed Captions: Enable All, Story Only, or Off, Size - Small to Huge, Font selection, Background Opacity, Speaker Names Toggle, Speaker Color Coding, Max Lines 1 to 4, Direction horizontal or vertical.
- CC for SFX: Footsteps, Music, etc. with size and opacity.
- Dialogue System: Typewriter Speed - Instant to Slow, Auto-Advance Delay, Skip Unread Toggle, Dialogue History Log Size.
- Floating Text: Damage Numbers, Heal Numbers, Quest Text - Scale, Opacity, Animation.
- Chat Text: Font Size, Opacity, Timestamp Toggle, Profanity Filter with custom word list import, Text-to-Speech for chat, Speech-to-Text transcription.
- Localization: Separate Text Language and Voice or Audio Language. Fallback font for CJK, Cyrillic, Arabic, Devanagari.
- Text-to-Speech or Speech-to-Text: TTS Toggle, Voice Selection, Speed 0.5x to 2x, STT for voice chat transcription toggle and language.
- Subtitle Speaker Color Coding: Unique colors per speaker.
- Subtitle Direction: Horizontal versus vertical layout for CJK.
- Dialogue History: Log buffer size and scrollback limit.
- Floating Combat Text: Scale, opacity, and animation speed.
- Chat Profanity Filter: Toggle with custom word list.
- Speech-to-Text: Real-time voice chat transcription.
- Fallback Fonts: Explicit font fallback chain.

VISUAL ACCESSIBILITY
- Colorblind Modes: Off, Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia, Custom Matrix plus Strength slider 0 to 100 percent.
- High Contrast Mode: UI and World high contrast separate toggles.
- Reduce Flashing: Photosensitive Mode - limit flashes to less than 3Hz, Screen Shake Reduction.
- Motion Reduction: Disable parallax, reduce animations.
- Cursor Customization: Size, color, outline, and motion trail for visibility.

HUD UI AND PRESENTATION
- UI Scale: Master 50 percent to 200 percent.
- HUD Scale and Opacity: Separate from UI, HUD Borders or Safe Area.
- HUD Visibility: Toggle individual elements, Dynamic or Minimal HUD, HUD Motion or Wobble toggle.
- Crosshair: Style, Color picker RGB or Hex, Size, Outline thickness, Dynamic spread toggle.
- Safe Area or Overscan: Percentage-based adjustment per display.

SYSTEM AND MEMORY MANAGEMENT
- VRAM Limits: Target VRAM usage slider or Texture Budget in MB or GB, Show usage bar.
- System RAM Limits: Cap system memory consumption.
- Cache Management: Pre-load assets toggle, On-demand texture streaming, Model pre-caching, Shader Pre-compilation, Clear Shader Cache button.
- Stats Overlay: FPS, Frame Time Graph, GPU, CPU, VRAM usage, Latency.
- GPU Selection: Force dedicated GPU in multi-GPU or hybrid graphics laptops.
- CPU Thread Allocation: Specific thread count or Auto with utilization display.
- Render API Selection: DirectX 11, DirectX 12, Vulkan, Metal, OpenGL with feature-level selection.
- Shader Pre-compilation: Toggle background shader compilation; Clear Shader Cache button.
- Performance Impact Indicators: Visual bar or icon next to each setting showing Low, Medium, High, Very High cost.
- Auto-Configure or Recommended Settings: Hardware detection with one-click apply based on VRAM, GPU, and CPU.
- Per-Category Reset: Reset to default for individual sections without global reset.
- Benchmark: Built-in benchmark with frame-time graph, 1 percent and 0.1 percent lows, and comparison report.
- Stats Overlay Customization: Customizable OSD with FPS, frame time, GPU clock, VRAM usage, CPU usage, latency, temperature.

AUDIO I/O SETTINGS
- Output Device Selection: Choose the audio OUTPUT device from the
  enumerated list - Speakers, Headphones, DAC, or Audio Interface,
  plus System Default. Show the driver/backend and current format.
- Input Device Selection: Choose the audio INPUT device (microphone,
  line-in, or interface input) used for voice chat / capture.
- Per-Device Audio Settings: For the selected output/input device -
  sample rate (44.1 / 48 / 96 / 192 kHz), bit depth, channel count,
  buffer size, and exclusive vs shared / low-latency access where the
  driver supports it.
- Input Monitoring or Sidetone: Toggle and volume per input device,
  with latency compensation so the monitor matches.
- Default Device Handling: Auto-select the last-used device and react
  to device connect/disconnect without losing the chosen device.
- Volume Mixers: Numeric 0 to 100 sliders for Master, Music, SFX,
  Dialogue, UI, Ambient, Voice Chat.
- Spatial Audio: Stereo, 5.1, 7.1, HRTF, Dolby Atmos, Windows Sonic, 3D Audio.
- Background Audio: Play or Mute when window loses focus.
- Voice Chat Volume: Separate mixer slider.
- Audio Compression or Dynamic Range: Night mode or compression toggle for consistent dialogue.
- Dialogue Boost or Clarity: EQ preset or high-pass filter for voice frequencies.
- Microphone Gain: Per input device input-level/trim slider (in
  addition to monitoring above).

INPUT CONTROLS AND INTERACTION
- Input Device Selection: Choose which physical device / backend fills
  each input role - keyboard, mouse, gamepad/controller. Which
  devices are offered depends on the build: the native low-level
  backend is chosen at COMPILE TIME between SDL and asyncinput
  (see input.txt). asyncinput builds offer the raw low-latency
  keyboard/mouse path; SDL-backend or web builds offer what the SDL /
  browser path exposes.
- Key and Button Mapping: Remap keyboard, mouse, and controller to
  actions, with conflict detection and a per-device default mapping.
- Per-Device Settings: Sensitivity and deadzone can differ per device
  (mouse vs analog stick), per stick/trigger, and per profile, not
  one global value.
- Key Remapping: Full keyboard, mouse, and controller remapping with conflict detection.
- Controller Deadzone: Inner and outer deadzone sliders for analog sticks and triggers.
- Aim Sensitivity: Separate X and Y axis, scoped, vehicle, and menu navigation sensitivity.
- Mouse: Raw input / low-latency mode, sensitivity or DPI, read or
  polling rate, and a mouse acceleration toggle with a custom curve.
- Haptics or Vibration: Intensity slider and toggle per event type such as damage, environment, UI.
- Input Lag Reduction: Raw input toggle. On asyncinput builds this
  selects the raw device path; on SDL-backend builds it reports
  whether a raw path is available at all.

NETWORK AND ONLINE
- Region Selection: Manual server region with ping display.
- Packet Loss or Latency Display: In-game network graph overlay.
- Bandwidth Limit: Cap upload and download for background stability.

FILE SAVE AND CONTENT MANAGEMENT
- Auto-Save or Save Slots: Number of backup slots and interval.
- Cloud Save Sync: Toggle with conflict resolution local, cloud, or both.
- Mod Support: Mod loading order, compatibility check display, and asset override rules.
- Screenshot or Video Capture Settings: Resolution, format, codec, and save path.

UI AND USABILITY OF SETTINGS MENU
- Quality Presets: Low, Medium, High, Ultra, Custom - with Apply and Revert.
- Logical Grouping: Categories as above with search bar for settings.
- Preview: Live preview window or Side-by-side comparison.
- Tooltips: Every setting explains visual impact, VRAM cost, and performance cost.
- Profiles: Named save slots such as Laptop Battery or 4K Ultra, import and export.
- Search Function: Quick search to find specific settings.
- Recommended Settings: Auto-detection and recommendation based on hardware.
- Reset to Defaults: Per-category or global reset options.
- Apply Changes: Instant preview or Apply button to test changes. Apply or Revert with countdown timer.
- Settings Import and Export: Ability to save and load custom configurations.
- Accessibility in Menu: High-contrast menu theme, full keyboard navigation, and screen reader tags for all options.
